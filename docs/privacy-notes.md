# Privacy notes (public copy)

Use this checklist before filling README placeholders or adding pages.

## Do not put on this public repo

- IRB raw materials, protocol text, or IRB numbers as “credentials”
- Clinical framing or health claims about participants / patients
- Participant data, field notes, transcripts, or de-identified datasets that are still local-only
- Links to private lab repos or private landscape work
- Anything you would not put on a public LinkedIn post

## What belongs here

- Public-safe wording about role, dates, and skills — only after you confirm it
- Research throughlines only when you have decided **if** they connect to Stealth and **what** can be said without oversharing
- Process / learning language over unverified outcomes

## Founder action

Fill public-safe wording yourself. If unsure whether a detail is public, leave the placeholder until you decide.
